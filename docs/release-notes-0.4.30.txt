Betspapa now fits every phone, from small iPhones to the Xiaomi 17 Pro, and fills the open Galaxy Z Fold and tablets with two-column cards. Picks, Slips and Codes no longer run off narrow screens, match times no longer hit the crests, and Copy and Load stay on one row. Your Trends slip shows both clubs, the pick, kick-off and price on one line, with crests or club initials. Large system text no longer breaks the layout, and buttons are easier to tap.
