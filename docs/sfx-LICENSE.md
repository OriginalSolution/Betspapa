# Betspapa notification sound effects

All five sounds were synthesized entirely in Python (band-limited oscillators, noise, envelopes, filters, light saturation, compression and short reverb) by TraffiAlert GH's assistant for Riddim Boss. No samples, downloaded audio, paid services or voices were used. Licence: original work, CC0 / free to use commercially.

- **red_card.ogg** — A synthesized pea-whistle (dual 2.8–3.2 kHz cavities with a fast warble/trill and breath noise) followed by a tense minor-second brass-like stab (F/Gb cluster) with a falling tail and a mid-range punch. CC0 / free to use commercially.
- **var_check.ogg** — A dramatic review / suspense build: opening question-tone blips, accelerating mid-range ticks, a rising filtered pulse and air riser, ending on an unresolved tritone hang and a soft downward scan blip. CC0 / free to use commercially.
- **penalty.ogg** — Two short referee-style tweets, then a rising hype riser into a punchy mid-range impact (body thump, crack, and a bright major-fifth brass stab). CC0 / free to use commercially.
- **goal_alt_1.ogg** — A bright stadium-horn-style fanfare made from layered, detuned synth brass in D major (two stabs then a held tonic chord with vibrato and grit). CC0 / free to use commercially.
- **goal_alt_2.ogg** — An energetic mid percussive hit (thump plus a short clap) followed by an accelerating rising D-major arpeggio of bright synth plucks, resolving on a sparkle chord. CC0 / free to use commercially.

Reproduce with `python3 make_sfx.py`. Sample rate is 44.1 kHz mono OGG Vorbis, peak-normalised to about −3 dBFS.
