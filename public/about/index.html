About Betspapa

Betspapa is a football matchday board. It shows the day’s real kick-offs and live scores, and alerts you when something happens in a match you follow. There is no simulated matchday: if the feed is unavailable, the board stays empty and says so.

Today

A week of kick-off cards, sorted by time, with today selected first. Followed teams and matches are pinned to the top, an in-play match shows a minute ring, and All, Live, Upcoming and Finished filters keep their counts. Search finds teams, leagues and matches, with trending teams and the next kick-offs one tap away.

Live and Following

Live scores, VAR decisions on the minute, and a match timeline. Follow a team or a single match to keep it at the top of the board.

Trends

Every tracked rate is shown with its real record, for example 8 of the last 10. Developing rows show form that is turning before the rate qualifies, and the last five results sit beside each trend.

Alerts

Alerts for kick-off, goals, VAR, red cards, half-time and full-time on the matches you follow. On Android the alert is a Betspapa card with both crests, the score and the match minute. A goal plays the cheer you choose in Settings, and tapping the alert opens that match.
