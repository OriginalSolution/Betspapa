Crests are resolved in this order and saved in this folder:

1. A logo URL on the fixture, when the feed actually sends one. The public source label stays Betspapa fixtures.
2. TheSportsDB team badge (free API key 123, searchteams.php and lookupteam.php). https://www.thesportsdb.com/
3. Wikidata P154 logo image, served from Wikimedia Commons.
4. The Wikipedia football-club page image.
5. For a national team with no badge: the federation crest from Wikidata, or the country flag. Bundled flags are flag-icons (MIT). Missing flags come from https://flagcdn.com/.

Manual fixes go in overrides.json under byName (normalized team name) or byId (team slug), pointing at a file in this folder or an https image URL. Teams that still have no confident badge are listed in missing.json.

SofaScore is not used. A bookmaker is not shown as the source of a crest.
