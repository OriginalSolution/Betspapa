Delete your data

Betspapa has no accounts. There is no username or password to close.

What we store

  • A push token from Firebase, plus the matches, teams and alert settings you chose, so the phone can receive match alerts. It identifies the app install, not you.
  • Pro purchase status, if you subscribed in the Android app. That record says the purchase is active. It does not include a card number. Google Play keeps the payment.

Follows and settings on betspapa.com stay in your browser. Clearing the site data in the browser removes them. We do not have a copy of that browser storage.

Delete it in the app

Open the Betspapa app, go to More, then Settings, then Delete my data. That unregisters this phone’s push token from our server. Alerts to that token stop. Turning notifications on again can register a new token.

Ask us to delete it

Email meditusa2023@gmail.com and include the device or the purchase information you have (for example the phone model and the approximate date you turned alerts or Pro on). We will remove the matching push registration and Pro purchase status within 30 days.

Uninstalling the app also stops alerts. The server registration is deleted when Firebase reports the token as no longer valid, or sooner if you use Delete my data or email us.
