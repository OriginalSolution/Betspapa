Get Betspapa for Android

The Betspapa app brings the matchday board to your phone, with alerts that arrive even when the app is closed.

Download the latest APK

Requirements

  • Android 7.0 or newer.
  • Free to download and use.

Installing the APK

  1. Download the APK on your phone.
  2. Open it and allow your browser to install apps if Android asks.
  3. Open Betspapa and allow notifications so goal and full-time alerts can reach you.

Alerts in the background

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. If alerts arrive late, open Betspapa → Settings and follow the battery steps for your phone.

Prefer the browser? Open the board on betspapa.com.
