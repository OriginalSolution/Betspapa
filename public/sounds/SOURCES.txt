Betspapa goal-cheer SFX — origin and licences
=============================================

All three files were synthesized on this machine. No third-party
audio samples or copyrighted loops were used. Safe for commercial
use in the Betspapa score app.

Shared tools
------------
- NumPy / SciPy (synthesis, filtering, WAV I/O)
- ffmpeg / libmp3lame (true-peak / LUFS metering, 48 kHz 192 kb/s MP3)
- Masters: 48 kHz, stereo, 32-bit float WAV; sample peak ~-8.0 dBFS;
  true peak no hotter than -7.9 dBTP.

cheer-crowd
-----------
Fully original procedural synthesis (Python). Layered brown/pink
noise, ~36 detuned formant-filtered "voice" layers, a shared open
vowel "YAAAH" wall, rising whoops, sine-vibrato whistles, and
scattered clap transients, plus a short stadium-style synthetic
reverb IR. No TTS and no sampled recordings.

cheer-afro
----------
Fully original procedural synthesis (Python) at 118 BPM. Synthesized
kick, talking-drum (pitch-bend toms), shaker grains, claps, gankogui-
style iron-bell partials, and a short C-mixolydian synth-brass riff
(band-limited saws + filter envelope). No sampled drums or horns.

cheer-chant
-----------
Shouted "GOAL!" voice:
  - eSpeak NG 1.52.0, voices en-us+m3 (lead) and en-us+m2 (double).
  - Text: "Gooaal!" plus a phoneme-held [[g'oUuuul]] body take.
  - Software / voice data licence: GNU GPL v3 (or later), Debian
    packages espeak-ng and espeak-ng-data 1.52.0+dfsg-5+b2.
    Copyright: Jonathan Duddington, Reece H. Dunn and contributors.
    The GPL covers the TTS engine and its voice data, not a recorded
    human performance. The rendered speech was further processed
    (resample to 48 kHz, mild time-compress, EQ, saturation, pitch
    layering) and is used here as an original game sting.
  - Additionally, an original Klatt-style formant-synthesized /ɡoʊl/
    shout (Python, no third-party voice) is layered under the TTS.

Bed: original synthesized kick + talking drum, formant crowd swell,
slapback echoes, and a synthetic stadium reverb IR.

Not used (explicitly avoided): Microsoft Edge TTS, Abena, F5-TTS Kafui,
or any other third-party recorded / neural voice.

cheer-papa
----------
Papa Shout recorded by the Betspapa owner, used with permission.
About 3.0 s, 48 kHz stereo, peak near −8 dBFS, loudness-matched to
the other cheers. The phone notification uses the Ogg file. In-app
Preview plays the MP3 at the volume slider.
