Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy covers the Betspapa Android app (package com.betspapa.scores) and the website betspapa.com. Betspapa is published by Medit USA LTD, Accra, Ghana (“we”). If you have any question about this policy, email meditusa2023@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • To send match alerts, the app sends our server a push token and the matches, teams and trends you follow.
  • We don’t store your IP address with that data, and we don’t sell your data.

What the app sends to our server

When notifications are turned on, the app registers with api.betspapa.com so we can send alerts to your phone even when the app is closed. That registration holds:

  • A push token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging. It identifies the app install for delivering notifications, not you.
  • What you follow: match ids, team ids and names, followed trends, and tips you have booked (their id and price), so we know which alerts to send.
  • Your alert settings: which events you want (kick-off, goals, cards, VAR, half-time, full-time, promotions) and the goal sound you chose.
  • App version and country. The country comes from our hosting provider’s network header, or a country-only lookup. We keep only the country, never the IP address.
  • Delivery records: the ids of alerts already sent to that token and when it was last updated, so you don’t get the same alert twice.

Everything else (follows, blacklists, alert history, preferences) stays on your device in the app’s local storage.

The website

betspapa.com needs no sign-up. Follows and settings you choose on the website are kept in your browser’s local storage. The website uses no advertising or analytics cookies. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives the standard request details your browser sends. The Play app may show ads; the website does not.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to your phone.
  • Google Play Billing handles a Betspapa Pro subscription if you buy one. Google processes the payment. We store whether that purchase is active, not your card number.
  • Google AdMob may show a banner and an optional rewarded video in the Play app. Google may use the Android advertising ID for that. Ads are not on the website. We do not put an ad next to a booking-code Copy button.
  • Render hosts our server (api.betspapa.com). Like any host it may keep short-lived technical request logs.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and the app download.

We don’t sell personal data. AdMob is the advertising service in the Play app. We don’t sell data to data brokers.

How long we keep it

A registration is kept while the app keeps using it. When Firebase reports a token as no longer valid (for example after you uninstall the app), we delete that registration.

Deleting your data

There are no accounts. The delete your data page says what we store and how to remove it.

  • In the app, open More, then Settings, then Delete my data. That unregisters this phone’s push token.
  • Or email meditusa2023@gmail.com with the device or purchase information. We remove the matching push registration and Pro purchase status within 30 days.
  • Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove what is kept on the phone.

Age

Betspapa shows football betting trends and bookmaker booking codes. It is for people aged 18 and over, and is not directed at children. Betting can be addictive, so please bet responsibly and only where it is legal for you.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Our server stores registrations without names, emails, phone numbers or IP addresses.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
